🎉 Extreme AI Voice Typer v4.6 is here! This update brings highly requested features, smarter AI handling, and better stability to make your typing experience flawless.

✨ What's New:
1. Universal Conversion Mode: We have made our conversion mode much smarter. It perfectly mixes scripts by writing English words in Roman A to Z alphabet and native words in your selected language script within the same sentence.
2. Custom AI Provider: Introducing the brand new Custom Provider feature! You can now connect any compatible AI provider by simply entering its Base URL and API Key. You can easily find this by opening AI Settings and choosing Custom Provider from the Provider dropdown menu.
3. Permanent Custom Models: You can now fetch or manually add your favorite custom models. Just open AI Settings, click on the Select Model dropdown, and choose + Add Custom Model at the bottom. These models will be permanently saved so you do not have to add them again.
4. Advanced AI Translation Control: We have introduced a powerful new translation control feature in Other Settings. You can now force the app to strictly use your selected AI provider for higher quality translations, or turn it off to use Google Translate as the primary fast engine.
5. Read Typed Text Aloud: We have added a new Read Typed Text Aloud feature in Other Settings. When you turn this on, the app will use text to speech to read aloud the exact text it just typed. If you turn it off, the typed text announcement will stop completely.
6. Smart Token Tracker: We have added a new token view inside AI Settings so you can easily check exactly how many tokens you have used. The app also automatically adjusts the token limits for thinking models to ensure you always get complete typing without errors.
7. Quick Settings Upgrades: Quick Settings is now more customizable! We fixed minor bugs like the language dropdown menu overlapping issue. You can now also add shortcuts for AI Settings and the new Read Typed Text Aloud feature directly in your Quick Settings panel.
8. API Key Privacy: We have added a Show and Hide toggle button for your API keys in the AI Settings menu to keep your private keys completely secure from screen readers or screen recordings.
9. Send Log to WhatsApp: You can now send error logs directly to the developer WhatsApp chat. This makes it much easier to discuss the problem and get instant help.

🛠️ Improvements:
1. Much Faster Performance: We have removed the long API waiting times. The network timeout is now optimized to 30 to 40 seconds, saving you time if a server goes down.
2. Crucial Bug Fixes: Fixed text encoding issues affecting waving emojis, native full stops, and the clear all voice commands for Hindi and Urdu languages.
