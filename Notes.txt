🎉 Extreme AI Voice Typer v4.5 is here! A massive leap forward in making AI typing truly effortless, fast, and powerful for everyone.
✨ What's New:
1. Introducing 'Extreme AI' Provider! Zero Setup Required. We’ve added our very own "Extreme AI" provider. Users who don't want the hassle of creating and managing API keys can simply select "Extreme AI" and start typing instantly. No keys needed, no complicated setup! The new Extreme AI provider comes pre-loaded with the 20+ fastest and most accurate models available today, including DeepSeek, Google Gemini, Groq, Qwen, and Microsoft. When selected, the settings interface automatically adapts and hides unnecessary fields, keeping your screen clean and distraction-free. Note: As the Extreme AI provider is newly introduced, you might experience minor issues or hiccups. We will continuously improve it in future updates.
2. Floating Dictation Button. Typing is now always at your fingertips! Enable the new floating "Start Dictating" button in settings to trigger AI Dictation instantly from anywhere on your screen. Double-tap to dictate, or long-press to quickly open Settings. You can quickly enable this floating button by navigating to General Settings, Other Settings.
3. Extreme AI Quick Controls, Quick Settings. Access your most-used features instantly! The new Quick Settings menu lets you toggle Smart Emojis, Translation, Typing Modes, and Languages right at startup. You can even fully customize which options appear in this menu for a personalized experience. You can easily enable and control Quick Settings by navigating to General Settings, Other Settings.
4. Advanced Voice Commands and "Clear Text" Feature. We’ve added a dedicated "Enable Punctuation Voice Commands" toggle in General Settings for better control over your dictation. Plus, introducing a powerful new command: simply say "Clear all" or "Clear text" to instantly erase everything in the typing box! The previous two voice commands have also been improved for better accuracy. You can now check all available commands by navigating to General Settings, Voice Commands.
5. Smart Error Logging System. We've introduced a new error tracking feature. Important: The extension automatically creates a small .txt log file in your device's internal storage to track performance. Please do not delete this file. If you ever face an error, bug, or typing issue in the future, sending this log will help the developer instantly pinpoint the exact root cause and fix it for you quickly. You can easily send this log file directly from the main General Settings menu by clicking the "SEND ERROR LOG TO DEVELOPER" button.
6. Unified Help and Support Menu. To make navigation cleaner and easier, the previous "About" and "Contact Us" buttons have been merged into a single "HELP AND SUPPORT" menu within General Settings. All helpful resources, including feedback and tutorials, are now neatly organized under this one section.
7. Support the Developer. If you are happy with Extreme AI Voice Typer and want to support the developer in making future updates even better, you can now easily make a contribution! Simply navigate to General Settings, HELP AND SUPPORT and click on the "Donate to Developer" button to pay your desired amount.
🛠️ Improvements:
1. Streamlined Interface: The "Use default API key" checkbox has been completely removed because we have now introduced the "Extreme AI" provider. If you do not want to create or manage your own API keys, you can simply use this new provider. Please keep in mind that since this provider is newly introduced, you might notice minor hiccups in typing performance initially, but it will keep getting better.
2. 'Conversion Mode' has been significantly enhanced for better script mixing and overall accuracy.
3. All newly added models are heavily optimized to strictly follow the rules of 'Conversion Mode' and 'Professional Writer Mode'.
4. Smoother and faster provider switching between Extreme AI, Groq, Gemini, and OpenRouter.
5. Added support for two new UI languages: Spanish and Farsi.
6. Various minor bug fixes and under-the-hood improvements have been made based on user feedback to provide a flawless and better typing experience.
💬 Got feedback or a feature request? We'd love to hear from you! Reach out directly through WhatsApp or the feedback form in General Settings, HELP AND SUPPORT.
