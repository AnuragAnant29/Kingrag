What's new in Extreme AI Voice Typer version 3.8.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
✨ NEW FEATURES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. SIMPLER UPDATE PROCESS.
◦ When a new update is available, you'll now see a clear, short message showing the new version and your current version, with an easy "Update Now" or "Cancel" choice. After updating, simply restart the plugin manually to apply the changes, and you'll automatically see what's new.

2. IMPROVED AI MODELS.
◦ Gemini and Groq models have been upgraded for better and smoother typing.

3. AUTO-SWITCH MODELS SIMPLIFIED.
◦ The "Auto-Switch Models" button has been removed. This feature now stays on by default, so you don't need to manage it yourself.

4. SMART ENGINE MOVED TO AI SETTINGS.
◦ The Smart Engine (AI Fallback to Raw Text) option is now available inside AI Settings. If the AI service ever fails or is unavailable, this feature makes sure your spoken words are still typed as plain text instead of being lost.
