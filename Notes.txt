🎉 Extreme AI Voice Typer v4.0 is here! A big update focused on flexibility, reliability, and making your typing experience smoother than ever.

✨ What's New:

1. OpenRouter AI Provider Is Back!
OpenRouter was removed earlier for a cleaner setup experience, but it's now back, with improvements! You can now use OpenRouter for typing too, giving you even more flexibility in choosing your AI provider.

2. Mistral AI Provider Removed
Based on user feedback, Mistral was causing typing issues for many users, so it has been removed and replaced with OpenRouter, which now offers reliable, high-quality typing.

3. Powerful New Voice Commands
Just say "Type in English" and speak in any language — it'll be typed out in clean, professional English instantly. Want another language? Say "Type in [any language]" (like Urdu, Spanish, or French) and watch it type exactly what you need, without switching any settings.

4. Fully External Language Files
Hindi and Urdu interfaces now load from separate language files, making future translations faster, easier to update, and more reliable than ever.

5. Smarter, More Reliable AI Model Handling
Behind-the-scenes upgrades ensure your recommended AI model stays active as long as it's working — no more unnecessary switching. If a model genuinely stops working, the app automatically picks the next best option, so your typing never skips a beat.

6. Clearer Update Experience
Updates are now simpler and more transparent. You'll see exactly what version is available, and after updating, a quick manual restart brings you right into what's new.

7. Smoother First-Time Setup
New users now get a clean, step-by-step onboarding experience, with clear guidance at every stage.

🛠️ Improvements:
• AI Settings interface is now cleaner. Selecting your AI provider (Gemini, Groq, or OpenRouter) automatically shows only that provider's API key field, along with a "Test API Key" button, right where you need it.
• Offline Mode still has your back. Even without internet or if the AI service is unavailable, your spoken words are still typed as plain text instead of being lost.
• Better handling of missing or unavailable language files, with clear on-screen alerts.
• Refined AI prompts for more natural, accurate voice command results.
• General performance and stability improvements across the app.
